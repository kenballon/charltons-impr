Biography

Sunny is a solicitor in the Hong Kong office. Sunny’s practice focuses on cross-border corporate and commercial transactions, including mergers and acquisitions, private equity investments and corporate reorganisation matters.

Experience

  • Acted for a PRC private equity firm in its proposed investment in a platform that acquires and grows Chinese third-party Amazon vendors
  • Acted for an American private equity firm in its proposed acquisition of a global provider of integrated marketing, packaging, print and supply chain solutions
  • Acted for a PRC investment bank in its preference shares investment into a leading medical device provider in China and the subsequent redemption of such shares
  • Advised a PRC leisure and tourism corporation on its acquisition of various Hong Kong based corporations, including but not limited to a logistics services group and a clubhouse management services provider
  • Acted for a PRC distressed asset management company in its disposal of various non-performing loans through public auctions
  • Advised multinational corporations on various cross-border corporate reorganisation matters, including but not limited to, dividend distribution, capital reduction, share redemption and deregistration
  • Bachelor of Science (Economics) (University College London)
  • Graduate Diploma in Law (BPP Law School)
